How it works

Four steps, one truth.

Water carries gold until it slows. Everything in the game follows from that.

The four steps

  1. 1Dig

    Steer the ring. Cold blue means nothing nearby. Hot orange means rich gravel. Dig there.

  2. 2Swirl

    Hold the pan under the water and swirl until the gravel settles. Gold sinks first.

  3. 3Wash

    Tilt gently and wash the light gravel over the lip. Stop when the black sand shows.

  4. 4Pick

    Tap each flake. Some pans hold a nugget.

Where gold settles

Read the water before you dig.

  1. Riffles

    The downstream side of stones, where the current breaks.

  2. Pools

    Where the current dies and the gravel is deepest.

  3. Bays

    Backwaters closed by a rock point.

  4. The pay streak

    One hidden in every stream. The richest gravel you will find.

Read the ring.

The ring is a detector. It glows cold blue over empty gravel, turns green, then gold, then hot orange as the gold beneath it gets richer, and it beeps faster as it warms. Violet means something solid that isn't gold: dig there too.
