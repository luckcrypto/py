Press

Press kit.

Pay Streak is a gold panning game in a clear mountain stream. Every stream is built from a seven-letter code, and every stream is new. Steer the detector ring to where the current slows, dig, swirl, wash and pick your gold, then work the claims of the gold rush trail.
