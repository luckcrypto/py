The gold rush trail

Work a claim. Earn your stars.

Each claim is one stream with one goal and a few pans. The trail map shows how far you have come.

How the trail works

  • Claims

    Each claim sets a goal and a number of pans. Finish with pans to spare for three stars, and every pan you don't need pays out as bonus gold.

  • Quests

    Three run at a time. Each one finished opens a chest.

  • Chests

    Wooden, iron and golden. Gold inside, and sometimes a booster. One more lies buried somewhere in every stream.

  • Boosters

    Golden Pan doubles the grains. Magnet picks every flake at once. Steady Hands stops wash-overs. Lucky Charm adds a nugget.
