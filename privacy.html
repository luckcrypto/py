Privacy

Nothing you do leaves your device.

Pay Streak collects no data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

What the game keeps, and where

To pick up where you left off, Pay Streak saves your progress on your own device: in the app's own storage on your phone. Nobody else can see it, including us, because Pay Streak never sends it anywhere. If you back up your phone, the phone's own backup (iCloud on an iPhone, Google's backup on Android) may include the app's data; that backup is between you and your phone's maker.

This website

The stream behind these pages is drawn for each visit from a fresh code, which your browser keeps until you close the tab. Nothing else is stored. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and load their typeface from Google Fonts, and like any web host, both receive your browser's request for the files.

Removing it

Deleting Pay Streak from your phone deletes everything it saved. Your progress does not sync between devices and is not backed up by us, though your phone's own backup may include it.

Children

Pay Streak collects no data from anyone, of any age.
